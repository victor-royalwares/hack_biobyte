## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an Afro-Culinary Hospitality aesthetic tailored for modern food-tech, ingredient logistics, and premium dining intelligence. It merges the warmth, vitality, and deep botanical heritage of West African food culture with the uncompromising precision of enterprise SaaS. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a clean, warm culinary atmosphere: inviting rather than clinical, yet rigorously systematic. The visual language blends:
- **Culinary Modernism:** Generous, breathable warm canvases, crisp structured cards, and structured culinary metadata.
- **Botanical Craft:** Rich rainforest greens evoke fresh produce, indigenous herbs, and deep culinary roots.
- **Warm Gastronomic Accents:** Palm-oil golds and sun-dried terracotta introduce appetite appeal, directional focus, and high-energy value metrics (savings, yield, nutritional density).

The emotional tone balances high-craft hospitality with fintech-grade reliability. Users feel the sensory richness of an upscale kitchen alongside the operational mastery of an industrial culinary dashboard.

## Colors
The palette captures Nigeria’s rich agricultural landscape and warm culinary palette against a pristine editorial background.

- **Primary (`#1B4332`)**: Deep Nigerian Rainforest Green. Used for primary actions, top-level brand markers, high-emphasis interactive surfaces, and key navigation structures.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F`)**: Mid Forest Green. Used for secondary active states, supporting tags, culinary category highlights, and active filter states.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Warm Palm-Oil Amber. Reserved for high-value transactional metrics, monetary savings, promotions, and critical nutritional callouts. Paired alongside a supporting terracotta tone (`#E07A5F`) for metabolic highlights, spicy index markers, and caloric metrics.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF9F6`)**: Warm Off-White. Replaces sterile cool greys with the soft warmth of natural linen, parchment, and raw cassava flour.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for raised component cards, data tables, and floating sheets to maintain crisp contrast against `#FAF9F6`.
- **Text & Structure (`#111827`)**: Deep slate for maximum legibility and ink-like editorial authority across all data-dense views. Border transitions leverage a quiet `#E5E7EB` to keep the structure grounded without visual clutter.

## Typography
The typography system balances editorial culinary character with technical metric precision:

- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue):** Brings geometric presence, subtle personality, and an organic contemporary weight. Perfect for culinary editorial storytelling, dish titles, and kitchen category anchors.
- **Body Copy (Hanken Grotesk):** Highly legible, humanistic grotesque offering effortless readability across dish compositions, provenance descriptions, and procurement manifests.
- **Labels, Values & Tabular Numbers (Space Grotesk):** Used for micro-copy, ingredient labels, yield percentages, protein gram counters, and Nigerian Naira (`₦`) pricing. Its technical construction guarantees high clarity for culinary specifications and supply-chain logistics.
- **Numeric Handling:** All instances of currency, macronutrients, and inventory ratios must apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee strict horizontal alignment in dense analytical tables.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a disciplined fluid grid system underpinned by an 8px spatial cadence (with a 4px sub-grid for tight metric lockups).

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (1200px+): 12-column fluid grid, 2rem canvas margin, 1.25rem column gutters. Maximum content constrain of 1440px.
  - Tablet (768px - 1199px): 8-column layout, 1.5rem margin, 1rem gutter.
  - Mobile (< 768px): 4-column layout, 1rem margin, 0.75rem gutter.
- **Spatial Rhythm:**
  - `space-xs` (4px) & `space-sm` (8px): Used strictly for badge padding, metric-to-label spacing, and micro ingredient tags.
  - `space-md` (16px): Internal cell padding for compact table listings and interactive element grouping.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Standard internal padding for cards, form fields, and modal containers.
  - `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks between menu categories, operational metrics, and seasonal highlights.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on crisp surface layering and botanical undertones rather than generic grey drop shadows.

- **Surface Hierarchy:**
  - Base Floor: `#FAF9F6` (canvas).
  - Resting Card Tier: Solid `#FFFFFF` bordered by `#E5E7EB` (1px stroke), elevated with an ultra-soft botanical ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.02)`).
  - Hover & Interactive Tier: Shift shadow to a warm diffused lift (`box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.03)`), maintaining structural boundary lines.
  - Overlays & Drawers: Elevated with a deep forest vignette (`box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.06)`).
- **Culinary Depth Accents:** Key operational cards (such as flash yield margins or seasonal harvest discounts) use a 2px left border accent in `#D97706` (Palm Amber) or `#1B4332` (Forest Green) to anchor hierarchy without introducing layered clutter.

## Shapes
The design adopts a **Soft (1)** shape language:
- Core UI elements (inputs, standard buttons, data cells) use `0.25rem` (4px) corners.
- Structural cards, media tiles, and modal bodies utilize `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px).
- Highlight panels, promotional callouts, and floating snackbars use `rounded-xl` at `0.75rem` (12px).
- Nutritional micro-chips, botanical pill indicators, and avatar cutouts leverage full capsule rounding (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an intentional counter-balance to sharp, architectural data grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1B4332` background with `#FAF9F6` text, 4px border radius. Hover state transitions to `#2D6A4F` with active scale compression (`transform: scale(0.99)`).
- **Secondary / Operational:** White surface `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#E5E7EB` border, text `#111827`. Hover applies border color `#1B4332` and background `#FAF9F6`.
- **Tertiary Accent (Action / Order / Save):** Solid `#D97706` with white text, utilized for checkout, batch orders, or high-value actions.

### Cards & Data Containers
- White `#FFFFFF` foundation layered over the `#FAF9F6` background.
- Crisp 1px `#E5E7EB` perimeter boundary.
- Internal padding of `space-lg` (24px).
- Card headers feature clear separation: Epilogue bold title on the left, Space Grotesk tabular data or status badges on the right.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Default: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#E5E7EB` border and 4px radius. 
- Focus State: Crisp 1.5px border in `#1B4332` paired with an ambient 3px outer ring in `rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12)`. No cold blue outlines.
- Placeholder text in muted warm slate (`#9CA3AF`). Monospace/Space Grotesk numeric values when entering ingredient quantities (kg, g) or Naira pricing (`₦`).

### Chips, Pills & Nutritional Tags
- **Protein & Dietary Tags:** Compact capsule shape (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 10px`, typography `label-sm`.
  - Botanical/Veg: Light green tint (`#E8F5E9`) with `#1B4332` text.
  - Palm Energy/Calorie: Pale amber tint (`#FEF3C7`) with `#B45309` text.
  - Terracotta Highlight: Pale terracotta tint (`#FBE9E7`) with `#C85A32` text for spice level, allergy flags, or urgent stock alerts.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 4px rounded checkbox boxes and circular radios.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` surface with 1.5px `#D1D5DB` border.
- Active/Checked: Filled with `#1B4332` carrying a crisp white glyph checkmark or inner dot.

### Lists & Tabular Culinary Grids
- Clean divider lines in 1px `#F3F4F6`.
- Line items maintain `space-md` vertical padding with interactive row highlighting using `rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.02)`.
- Numbers (weights, yield rates, batch costs, and Naira values) are strictly formatted in `Space Grotesk` with `tabular-nums` right-aligned against alphabetical item descriptions.

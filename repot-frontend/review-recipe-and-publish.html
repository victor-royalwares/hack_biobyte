Jollof Rice & Soya Chunks

A savory, protein-rich plant-based twist on classic Nigerian jollof rice using seasoned soya chunks, aromatic tomatoes, peppers, and fermented locust beans (iru).

skillet

How to prepare

4 Steps · 20 Mins Prep
  1. 1

    Soak the soya chunks in hot water for 10 minutes.

  2. 2

    Squeeze out excess water.

  3. 3

    Fry with onions, pepper, and iru until lightly browned.

  4. 4

    Mix into your jollof rice pot and simmer for 5 minutes.
